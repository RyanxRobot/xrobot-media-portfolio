Tracker Suite

Two tools an operator uses on a live shift. Live Tracker runs the break schedule across five stations in three time zones and fires the hub gang roll. Shift Tracker turns what went wrong into a filed discrepancy report (DR). The tour walks through one missed break from start to finish.
